AppGuard Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2024

Data Collection

AppGuard does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with third parties. All analysis is performed locally on your device.

Permission Usage

The QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission is used solely to list and analyze applications installed on your device. No data leaves your device.

Third Party Services

The free version of AppGuard displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information in accordance with Google's privacy policy.

Pro Version

Pro subscriptions are processed through Google Play Billing. We do not store any payment information.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at: appguard.support@gmail.com
