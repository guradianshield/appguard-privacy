What's My Phone Risk - Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

What's My Phone Risk ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to transparency about how the app and its third-party services handle information.

1. Local App Analysis

The app accesses and analyzes installed application names, package information, installation sources, requested and granted permissions, and special access status to perform security scans and calculate risk scores. This analysis is performed locally on your device. Installed app information and scan results are not uploaded to the developer's servers or shared with advertising providers.

2. Permission Usage

QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES: Used only to list installed applications for the app's security scan.

INTERNET: Used to provide Google AdMob advertising in the free version and to communicate with Google Play services.

POST_NOTIFICATIONS and RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Used for optional scan reminders and scheduled scan-related notifications.

3. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

The free version uses Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Depending on device settings, region, consent choices, and ad availability, Google may automatically collect and share the following categories of information:

  • Approximate location inferred from the device IP address
  • App interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad or video views
  • Diagnostic information, such as app or SDK performance and reliability data
  • Device or other identifiers, including the Android advertising ID or app set ID when available

Google processes this information for advertising or marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Consent and Privacy Choices

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request or update privacy choices where required. Advertising is initialized only when the consent system indicates that ads may be requested. Depending on your region, you may be shown options concerning personalized advertising and related data processing.

You can reset or delete your Android advertising ID and manage advertising privacy settings in your Android device settings. You can also manage applicable Google advertising choices through My Ad Center. Pro subscribers do not receive ads from the app.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

The developer does not operate a server that stores installed app lists, scan results, advertising identifiers, or AdMob diagnostic data. Scan information stored by the app remains on the device and can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. Information processed by Google is handled according to Google's policies and user controls linked above.

6. Pro Purchases and Subscriptions

Monthly and yearly Pro subscriptions, as well as the one-time 24-hour Pro access purchase, are processed through Google Play Billing. The 24-hour option is a one-time payment, does not renew automatically, and provides Pro access for 24 hours from purchase. The developer does not collect or store payment card information. Google handles billing information under its own privacy policy.

7. Children's Privacy

What's My Phone Risk is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. Advertising and consent behavior may also be limited by applicable Google policies and regional requirements.

8. Security

Installed app analysis remains on the device. Data transmitted by supported Google services is encrypted in transit. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the app, third-party services, or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
