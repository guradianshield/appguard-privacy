AppGuard - Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2024

AppGuard ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles information.

1. Information We Collect

AppGuard does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data with third parties. All processing is performed locally on your device.

2. Permission Usage

QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES: This permission is required to list all installed applications on your device. AppGuard uses this permission solely to scan and analyze installed apps for security risks. No app data is transmitted outside your device.

INTERNET: Used only to display advertisements in the free version via Google AdMob. No personal data is transmitted.

3. Installed App Analysis

AppGuard scans installed applications and analyzes their permissions to calculate risk scores. This analysis is performed entirely on your device. No information about your installed apps is sent to our servers or any third party.

4. Advertising (Free Version)

The free version of AppGuard displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

5. Pro Subscription

Pro subscriptions are processed through Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store any payment information. All billing is handled by Google.

6. Data Security

Since AppGuard does not collect or transmit any personal data, there is no risk of data breach from our side. All analysis remains on your device.

7. Children's Privacy

AppGuard is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
